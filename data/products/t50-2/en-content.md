Heading: Sync Tilt. Description: The tilt — the chair's core mechanism —
precisely adjusts the angle of backrest and seat so you keep moving naturally

deciding measures of an office chair, finely to your build and the work at hand.
- **Synchronised tilting** — as you recline, the seat moves with the backrest

  back and pelvis closely supported. (video 7b3e9de3)
