## 9. Section: Natural leather characteristics

**Heading:** About natural leather

**Body:**
SIDIZ uses natural cowhide. Natural leather carries the traces of the animal's whole life. Scars,
wrinkles and variations in colour picked up as the animal grew are characteristics of natural
leather, not defects. They are the mark of something that was never produced artificially.

**⚠️ Please note:** Light-coloured natural leather can pick up colour transfer when rubbed against
dark clothing such as denim. This is not covered by exchange or refund.

### 1. There are marks or wrinkles on the leather surface
*Wrinkles / scratches · veins / sinew · insect bites*

Cattle hide carries marks large and small. They show the years the animal lived, and they do not
weaken the durability of the leather. If anything, they are proof that this is natural leather and
not synthetic or an imitation.

### 2. The texture of the leather feels different in places
*Variation in surface grain*

Wrinkle density and skin texture differ between the neck, back and belly of the hide, so the surface
grain is not uniform. Slight variation is a characteristic of natural leather.

### 3. The colour of the leather looks different in places
*Variation in surface colour*

Natural leather can vary slightly in colour depending on when it was dyed and which part of the hide
it came from. This is inherent to a natural material — it is not a fault, and is not covered by
exchange or refund. Colour can also appear different depending on the direction of sunlight or the
indoor lighting.

## 15. Section: FAQ

**What is the SIDIZ T90 designed for?**
The SIDIZ T90 is an ergonomic office chair designed around long hours of computer work. It is built
to respond flexibly to changes in posture and movement — particularly in modern multi-device
setups — so it works equally well for working from home, the office, or specialist work environments.

**How is the SIDIZ T90 different from an ordinary office chair?**
The T90 is a premium office chair built on performance engineering. Its flexible backrest, Perfect
Fitting Lumbar Support and 4D armrests respond to your body and your working posture, and the design
is recognised by seven registered industrial property rights.

**Will my back and neck still be comfortable after sitting for a long time?**
The T90 is an ergonomic chair designed to stay comfortable through long working days. Lumbar support
holds your lower back steady and the backrest moves naturally with you, helping you hold a good
posture and reducing the fatigue that builds up over a day.

**Who would you recommend the SIDIZ T90 to?**
The T90 suits people who spend most of the day at a desk — developers, designers, planners and
remote workers who are at a computer for long stretches.

**Is it suitable for working from home?**
Yes. The T90 suits home offices well. Beyond the design, it is built around a range of working
postures, so you get office-grade comfort and concentration at home. It also won a prize in the
office chair category at the iF Design Award 2025 in Germany.

**Can it be adjusted to fit my body?**
Yes. The T90 adjusts in detail to suit your body. Seat height and depth, backrest and armrests can
all be adjusted to build a seating setup optimised for you.

**What kind of work setup does the T90 suit?**
The T90 suits setups using multiple monitors, laptops and tablets. It provides stable support and
flexible movement even where posture changes frequently.

**Is it suitable for bulk orders for an office?**
Yes. The T90 is a premium office chair designed for professional work environments and suits
