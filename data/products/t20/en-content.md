## FAQ (22) — three items
1. Can I buy the headrest or seat separately? — Yes: when a part needs
   replacing, buy the Easy Repair part and swap it in easily.
2. How far does the backrest recline, and can it lock? — Up to 125°, and the
   backrest angle locks in three positions: 105°, 113° and 121°.
3. Tension will not adjust when the backrest is locked at step 2 or 3 — the
   tension lever is fixed in those positions; return the backrest upright to
   step 1 and adjust without leaning on it.
